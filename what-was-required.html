What Was Required

A colliery town in the age of candle and coal. One boy in a generation is raised out of the rows into the Company's offices — and climbs high enough to read what the top truly keeps in its books. A novel of pressure, legitimacy, and the price of speaking. Seven chapters. Every door opens both ways.

The Complete Novel

Chapters

Seven chapters. Read in order.

The Constraint

Chapter One

Nico is born at the bottom of things — Cinder Row, the low seam, the chain. Then the one door in the valley opens, and it opens for him.

The Language

Chapter Two

The office takes his grammar first. In the letter-books he learns where the air goes: words are stores, kept dry, issued at need.

The Obedience

Chapter Three

At the beam he docks his own street — and finds the screw. He reports it through the proper channel. The channel promotes him.

The Legitimacy

Chapter Four

In the agent's office he drafts the beautiful answers himself — and holds the pen in the warm room where the cost of the bottom is priced.

The Effort

Chapter Five

The top is a reading-room. Deaths per thousand tons. The scale of allowances found adequate. And a thirty-year-old minute with his own life in it.

The Silence

Chapter Six

Fire in the east workings. Forty-one men below. The board must choose between the pit and the men — and Nico holds the pen.

The After

Chapter Seven

What Nico does. The stores come out from under the shirt, by no channel of the Company's building — and the fair round hand stands in the dock.
