What Was Required

A colliery town in the age of candle and coal. A clerk at the Company's scale who knows what the beam reads, and what it should read. A novel of pressure, legitimacy, and the moment restraint fails. Seven chapters. No exits announced.

The Complete Novel

Chapters

Seven chapters. Read in order.

The Constraint

Chapter One

The candle had learned his hand. Marek, weigh-clerk of the Vesta pit, wakes into the arithmetic of Prosper Row.

The Language

Chapter Two

The men speak to the beam, because the beam cannot testify. On Sundays, the clerk translates the row's troubles into words an office will file.

The Obedience

Chapter Three

A tub laid out. A chain with no link where the wrong lives. And by lamplight, alone, a clerk discovers what the beam has been reading.

The Legitimacy

Chapter Four

A widow's petition is heard. A dying man's claim comes late by rule. And the men's memorial goes into the process, and the process digests it.

The Effort

Chapter Five

A tin under the floorboard. A sermon on diligence preached to hands already at full stretch. A winter that empties six years in one month.

The Silence

Chapter Six

A fifth off the timber where the roof is worst. A clerk waiting for the right moment. The fourth of September, and what an inquest records.

The After

Chapter Seven

Nothing collapsed. The pit turns, the rent is held — and the valley, without meeting or oath, quietly declines to extend the loan again.
